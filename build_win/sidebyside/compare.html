Method. Both binaries built with MinGW-w64 GCC 15, -O3 -flto, from a worktree at each commit — stock is the pristine upstream source plus only the minimal Windows-portability fixes needed to compile at all (no rendering-logic changes). 1200×900, 4× antialiasing, 1 thread, wall clock. 7 other scenes (including a synthetic 60,000-object stress scene and a transparency test) also verified byte-identical between stock and now; only the AO sampler algorithm change breaks bit-identity, by design, on ambient-occlusion scenes.
